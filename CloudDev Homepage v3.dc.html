What is mutation testing?

Faults (mutations) are automatically seeded into your code, then your tests are run. If a test fails, the mutation is killed; if all tests pass, it lived. Mutation coverage shows what your tests can actually catch — it's the gold standard. You just saw it happen to our headline.
